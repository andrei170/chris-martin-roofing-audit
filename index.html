🔴 Why Indeed Keeps Sending The Wrong People
🔴🔴 It is not bad luck, it is the channel

A qualified roofer with real experience is already employed. He is not browsing a job board on a Tuesday evening.

⭐⭐ So the only people who see your advert are the people currently out of work, and you are selecting from the part of the trade that is available rather than the part that is good. That is exactly the complaint you made, and it is the channel doing what it does.

⭐ Reaching a working roofer means interrupting him where he already is — a social feed, a local trade group, the merchant counter, or a word from somebody he knows.

⭐⭐ The cheapest thing you could do costs you nothing from us

Ask the lad who works with you. Good tradesmen know other good tradesmen, they know who is unhappy where, and a recommendation from somebody already on your books is pre-vetted in a way no advert can be.

⭐ Put money behind it. A referral payment, enough to matter, paid on arrival and again after a few months. We would tell you to try that before paying us anything.

🔴 Your website has a Meta pixel on it, and nothing for a roofer to read

We read chrismartinroofing.co.uk. Lancaster and Morecambe, 781 words, one enquiry form, two tap-to-call links. You have Google Analytics and a Meta pixel installed, which means somebody has run or prepared Facebook advertising.

🔴 But there is no careers page and nothing aimed at a tradesman — no team, no photographs of the work with names attached, nothing about what it is like to work there. ⭐⭐ A good roofer deciding whether to come and work for you will look you up, and there is nothing to find.

⚠️ And we found no accreditation on the site — no NFRC, CHAS, Checkatrade or TrustMark. That is not evidence you hold none. It is evidence a customer, or a roofer, cannot see them.

⚠️ Two main headings on one page

A small technical point: your homepage has two H1 headings where it should have one. ⚠️ It confuses search engines about what the page is actually about. Minutes to fix, and the sort of thing that gets left behind when a site is built in a hurry.

⚠️ Two people is a different problem from six

With two of you, one hire is a fifty per cent increase in the workforce. That is a much bigger decision than it sounds, and it is why getting the right person matters more to you than to a firm with eight.

⭐ It also means advertising badly is expensive in time, not just money. Every unqualified applicant you interview is time off a roof.

⭐ What We Are And Are Not
⛔ Before anything else

AG1 is a marketing company, not a recruitment agency. We will not interview anybody, assess a roofer's competence, check a reference or tell you who to hire. We cannot guarantee a hire or a number of applicants.

⭐ What we can do is make sure the right people see that you are looking, and that what they find gives them a reason to want it.

⚠️ And with two of you, we would be honest about scale. This is a small, focused piece of work, not a campaign. If a referral payment to your lad solves it, that is the right answer and it costs you nothing.

What We Would Want To Ask
  • 1. How many have you actually interviewed, and what was wrong with them? ⭐ Unqualified, or unreliable? Different fixes.
  • 2. What does your Indeed advert say, and does it state the pay?
  • 3. Employed or subcontract?
  • 4. What would the new person actually do day to day?
  • 5. Have you asked your lad whether he knows anyone? ⭐ And is there money attached to it?
  • 6. Who ran the Facebook advertising? ⭐ There is a Meta pixel on your site, so somebody set it up. Was it for customers, or did you try it for hiring?
  • 7. What changes in the middle of next year? ⭐ More work coming, or just a feeling that it is time?
⭐ Question 7 is the honest one. If nothing specific changes mid-year then there is no reason to wait. If something does, we should plan around it rather than ignore it.

Indeed reaches people who are out of work.

You want somebody who is in work, and happy to move for the right job.
